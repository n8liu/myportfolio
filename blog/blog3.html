My Gym Routine
May 20, 2025

Clearing My Mind Through Movement

Coding for hours can take a toll, not just on the posture, but also on mental clarity. When my code compiler throws incomprehensible errors or database queries run slow, the gym is my escape. It provides a structured space to disconnect, focus entirely on physical execution, and release stress.

Consistency in physical training has directly improved my academic focus. It instills a sense of routine and discipline that transfers perfectly to working through difficult computer science assignments or long debug cycles.

The Weekly Split

I follow a classic Push-Pull-Legs (PPL) split, training 4-5 times a week with active recovery days. This allows for optimal muscle group targeting and plenty of rest:

  • Push Days: Chest, shoulders, and triceps (focusing on bench press, overhead press, and lateral raises).
  • Pull Days: Back, rear delts, and biceps (deadlifts, pull-ups, and seated cable rows).
  • Leg Days: Quads, hamstrings, and calves (squats, leg curls, and calf raises).

Lessons in Consistency

Just like coding, progress in the gym doesn't happen overnight. It relies on progressive overload—small, incremental increases in weight and volume over weeks and months. Keeping a log of sets and weights helps track progress and keeps me accountable, much like committing code to a GitHub repository.
