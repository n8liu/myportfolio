# Ranking Every Matcha I Tried!

Matcha. The taste of grass, I love it. Here is every place I have tried and my thoughts.

## My Top 5
(Updated Aug. 20, 2026)

1. Airoma Cafe (Fountain Valley, CA): Matcha Einspanner -> big cup and actually tastes the matcha
2. Brew Story (Huntington Beach, CA): Banana Cream Matcha -> actually really good, decent cup, good flavors, strong matcha
3. Matsu Matcha (Cupertino, CA): Biscoff Matcha -> best biscoff matcha (i love biscoff)
4. Community Goods (Los Angeles, CA): Rocky's Matcha -> probably tastes the best but cups are a scam
5. Da Vien (Milpitas): Banana Matcha -> was my go to, good value, big cup
